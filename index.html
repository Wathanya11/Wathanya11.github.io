Other experience / COLA KKU for Community
Student volunteer · Campaign case study

R3 Ducka duck that sorts waste.

COLA KKU x COLAB invited students to volunteer with five local governments around Khon Kaen University. Our team was given Ban Pet, a subdistrict with a waste problem, and asked to design a campaign the community would actually use.

01

The problem

Ban Pet was facing a waste problem, and households needed a simple way to deal with it.

02

The goal

Instead of only asking people to make less waste, help every household handle its waste correctly, every day.

03

The idea

"Ped Yaek Laek Suk": a friendly duck mascot, named after Ban Pet, that turns sorting waste into something people feel good about.

My role

What I did for Ban Pet.

01

Campaign concept

Shaped the idea and message of the campaign around how people in Ban Pet live and sort their waste.

02

Logo design

Designed the duck mascot logo with four colour-coded bins, so the sorting rule is clear at a glance.

03

Community engagement

Planned ways to motivate villagers to join in and keep taking part, then helped pitch the campaign.
